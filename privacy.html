Privacy

Chitas · Last updated October 2026

The short version. Chitas keeps your learning progress so it can show it back to you. It has no ads, no tracking and no analytics services, and it never sells or shares your information. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app, at any time.

What we keep

  • Without an account: your progress and settings stay on your phone only.
  • With an account (Sign in with Google or Apple): your name and profile picture from that sign-in, and your email address (used only to sign you in), so your learning is saved and on all your devices.
  • Your learning: which sections you marked on which days, your streak and totals, milestones, Review results, and where you were reading.
  • Your settings: your Rambam track, translation and display choices, the extra learning you added, and, if you choose to fill it in, your city.
  • Personal Tehillim: if you add people, their names and Hebrew birthdays, so the app can show each person's chapter. Only you can see them.

Who can see it

Only you. Your account is protected so that no other user can read it. There are no public profiles and no leaderboards.

The app's maker sees only totals across everyone, such as how many people learned today or how many reviews were made, to know the app is working. Those totals never include anyone's name, email, city, Tehillim names or individual progress.

Services that help run the app

  • Google Firebase signs you in and stores your account.
  • Vercel runs the app's website and its small server.
  • Sefaria and Hebcal provide the texts and the Jewish calendar. Your phone asks them for the day's texts, as any website would.
  • Anthropic (Claude) writes the Review questions and the Tanya guide. It is only sent the text of the day's learning, never anything about you.

Like any website, these services may keep short technical logs (such as the IP address a request came from) to run and protect their systems.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile → Delete account. Your progress, settings and sign-in are deleted from our systems right away, and from your phone. Without an account, the same button clears everything stored on your phone.

Children

Chitas is a learning app suitable for all ages. It doesn't knowingly collect more than what's described here from anyone.
