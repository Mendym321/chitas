Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. You can learn without one, and your progress stays on your phone. Sign in with Google or Apple to keep it safe and see it on all your devices.

Which Rambam do you follow?
Three chapters a day, one chapter a day, or Sefer HaMitzvos. Change it any time in Profile.

Can I mark a day I missed?
You can mark today and the past seven days. You can read any day.

Where do the texts come from?
From Sefaria and Hebcal. See Profile → Texts and credits.

How do I delete my account?
Profile → Delete account. Everything is removed right away. See the privacy policy.
